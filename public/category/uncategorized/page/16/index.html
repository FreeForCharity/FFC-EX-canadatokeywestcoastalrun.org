Jul 12 2015

Day one in the books

Day one was an adventure! Left St. Stephen and made it the outskirts of Perry maine. Found a “unofficial” place to camp, and hunkered down for the night. Coastal rain arrived about 2:00am just to keep me humble, and I was wearing everything in my pack by morning….but I did get some sleep.

maine is beautiful, and I am taking pics when I can. The people have been very friendly and helpful.

day two happening now. trying to get to dennysville by nightfall…halfway there now, wish me luck! Free WiFi at this lone gas station….and the only opportunity for food till tomorrow so thought I would charge my phone and post.
